The Gateway Strategy: PYQ Analysis

Why PYQs are Gold?

In AKTU, the same core concepts are revisited every few years. Our Gateway section is designed to help you identify these "Gold Topics" that are virtually guaranteed to appear.

5-Year Mapping

Analyze the last 5 years of papers. If a topic has been missing for 2 consecutive years, it has a 90% chance of being asked in the next cycle.

Pattern Recognition

Notice how AKTU shifts from theory to numericals every few years. Use our PYQs to gauge the current "mood" of the question-setting committee.
